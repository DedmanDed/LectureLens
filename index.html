Why is self-attention useful in a transformer?

Self-attention lets each token weigh information from other tokens when building its representation. This helps the model capture relationships between words even when they are far apart in a sequence.

In this lecture, the available context introduces attention as a way to relate tokens rather than processing each position in isolation.
